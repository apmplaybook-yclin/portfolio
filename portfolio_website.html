About Me

Building products people love to use

I'm a product manager lead with roots in UX design, currently based in Taipei. My work spans user research, product strategy, and cross-functional collaboration — with a focus on building experiences that feel intuitive and meaningful.

Previously at Trend Micro, Yahoo, and NIO. I studied User Experience Design at the Illinois Institute of Technology.

Outside of product work, I find clarity through photography — whether chasing snow in Sapporo, wandering through Kathmandu, or exploring the streets of Taipei with a camera.
